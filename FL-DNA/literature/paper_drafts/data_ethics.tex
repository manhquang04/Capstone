\paragraph{Data and ethics.}
PaySim is a synthetic, agent-based mobile-money simulation calibrated from aggregated private logs, rather than a release of the original transaction records.  Its fraud labels therefore represent simulated injected behaviour and should not be interpreted as adjudicated operational fraud.  The dataset authors' description further warns that the four account-balance fields (\texttt{oldbalanceOrg}, \texttt{newbalanceOrig}, \texttt{oldbalanceDest}, and \texttt{newbalanceDest}) should not be used for fraud detection because detected fraudulent transactions are cancelled.  Since the present loader includes these fields and derived balance differences, we report a no-balance-feature ablation and avoid claims that depend solely on potentially leaky balance information.  PaySim is listed as CC BY-SA 4.0; additional dataset-page and Kaggle-platform terms remain unverified.  Adult is a historical Census-derived income benchmark containing sensitive or proxy attributes, including age, sex, race, marital status, relationship, occupation, and national origin.  Its income label is socially contingent and benchmark performance is neither a fairness assessment nor evidence suitable for high-impact decisions.  Adult is listed by UCI under CC BY 4.0.  We retain the required attribution, licence links, and change notices when sharing covered material; licensing beyond the verified terms is unverified.  Evaluations concern published defenses and use responsible-disclosure practices for any identified weaknesses.  No attacks target real users or real user data: PaySim is synthetic, and results are reported as controlled benchmark evidence rather than deployment claims.
