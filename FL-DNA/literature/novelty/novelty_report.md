# Novelty Audit: Positive-Control-First Evaluation of FL Gradient-Leakage Defenses

## Question and decision

**Question.** Is a systematic re-evaluation of Soteria, PRECODE, gradient pruning, ATS, count-sketch aggregation, and two seeded update transforms, using native image/tabular attacks and checks (a)--(d), novel enough to claim a new evaluation methodology?

**Short answer.** Not as a claim to be the first systematic re-evaluation or the first adaptive-defense audit. Huang et al., Balunovic et al., Yue et al., Li et al., Wei et al., Hatamizadeh et al., and the 2025 SoK already establish most of the underlying principles: strong/adaptive attacks, no-defense controls in many experiments, input-space image metrics, utility--privacy plots, random/marginal tabular controls, and BN-state leakage. The defensible contribution is narrower: a preregistered, reproducible **cross-defense audit protocol** that requires all four checks together, uses official native reference attacks in both image and tabular domains, explicitly represents the server's key/seed knowledge, and exposes a *specified, clipped* DP comparator under both distortion and utility matching. No reviewed work was found that executes that exact conjunction across the named defense classes. This is a scoped “not found,” not proof that no such work exists.

## Scope and evidence rules

Window: 2020--2026; sources: arXiv/OpenAlex through `literature`, Semantic Scholar targeted queries/retry, and the Breaching GitHub documentation. Exact searches, rate-limit/unavailability records, retained corpus, and exclusions are in [search_log.md](search_log.md). All claims below are based on passages read in the cited work. “No” means the reviewed paper does not report that item in its actual evaluation, not that it is impossible or absent from every reference it cites. “Not formal” means an undefended result appears but is not a prespecified pass/fail gate against data-free baselines.

Checks:

- **(a) Positive control:** an attack on undefended updates that must pass a declared, meaningful criterion before defense results are interpreted.
- **(b) Server-knowledge adaptation:** attack/evaluation matches information the server has, including defense parameters, labels, keys/seeds, model state, and transmitted state.
- **(c) Input-space/data-free calibration:** reconstruction is measured in input space and compared to a data-free guess (constant/mean image or marginal/mean tabular guess).
- **(d) DP comparator:** clipping is stated; DP is matched to the compared method both by update distortion and downstream utility.

## Strict gap analysis

### Already done

1. **The high-level audit premise is not new.** Wei et al. call their work an evaluation framework; Huang et al. systematically compare gradient pruning/encoding defenses with a strong configuration; Balunovic et al. formalize defense-aware Bayesian inference; Yue et al. demonstrate adaptive attacks against obfuscation; Li et al. audit four defense mechanisms with a stronger generative attack; Du et al. systematize practical GIA settings and post-processing defenses. A paper claiming “the first systematic re-evaluation of FL gradient defenses” would be false.
2. **Adaptive, information-rich attackers are established practice.** Huang et al. grant labels/BN statistics and even attacker-favorable encoding information; Hatamizadeh et al. require current checkpoint and BN evolution; Balunovic and Yue explicitly formulate attacks around the defense/obfuscation. A claim that using an adaptive attacker is novel would be false.
3. **Input-space reconstruction quality is established.** MSE/PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS and visual inspection are standard in image studies. TabLeak is especially close to planned tabular scoring: it provides a stated feature-accuracy metric and compares against an empirical per-feature marginal guess. A claim that image- or tabular-input scoring itself is novel would be false.
4. **Privacy--utility tradeoffs and DP/noise comparisons are established.** Huang reports utility for non-DP defenses; Yue and Du plot defense/accuracy tradeoffs; Hatamizadeh varies DP noise and testing accuracy; Balunovic and Li discuss/use clipping+noise. A claim that the project first compares defenses with DP or first reports utility would be false.
5. **BN state is a known leakage channel.** Hatamizadeh directly shows that correctly modeling updated BN running statistics is essential, and Huang assumes private-batch BN statistics for the strongest attacker. The project cannot claim first discovery of BN leakage.

### Not found in the reviewed corpus, and therefore defensible only with this wording

1. **All four checks as an enforced protocol.** None of the reviewed multi-defense works couples: a declared attack-positive-control gate; the exact server’s knowledge (including explicit seed/key availability); input-space comparison against a deliberately data-free baseline in both domains; and a DP comparator with its clipping rule stated and **both** update-distortion and downstream-utility matched. Individual components appear separately. This conjunction is the core methodological gap.
2. **Native, official reference attacks across image and tabular settings in one defense audit.** TabLeak provides the tabular native attack and data-free baseline; image papers provide Geiping/other attacks. No reviewed work executes a common defense audit using the official Geiping implementation in LeNet-Zhu/CIFAR-10 and official TabLeak in Adult as two fixed native positive controls. This is a reproducibility/protocol contribution, not an algorithmic novelty claim.
3. **Key-holding-server analysis of update transforms.** The reviewed works examine defense parameters and encoding knowledge, but no reviewed evaluation treats a server-held seed for a keyed update transform as an explicit attack input and compares server-known versus secret-key cases. This gap must remain scoped to the reviewed corpus; it is not evidence that no such cryptographic/privacy literature exists.
4. **DP comparator calibration at the update-coordinate level.** Existing studies either omit DP, use clipping/noise without showing both matches, or select points on an accuracy/leakage curve. The project’s planned requirement to publish clipping scope/norm and retain both distortion-matched and utility-matched DP controls is a useful reproducibility addition. It is not a new DP mechanism or a proof of a universal comparison principle.
5. **The exact defense coverage is new only as a suite.** Soteria and PRECODE have adaptive reevaluation precedent; pruning, clipping, sparsification and generic post-processing have broad precedent; count-sketch aggregation and the two project transforms are not jointly covered by the reviewed audits. Claim coverage of the bundle, not novelty of every member.

## Strongest honest positioning

This work should be positioned as a **reproducible evaluation protocol and case study**, not as a new gradient-inversion attack or the first critique of defense evaluation. Prior work has already shown that attack strength, implementation choices, defense-aware objectives, BN state, and utility--privacy tradeoffs can reverse conclusions. The contribution is to operationalize those lessons in a fixed, falsifiable four-check gate for defense claims: an attack must first work without defense; its information must match the observing server; output quality must beat a data-free input baseline; and the DP comparator must state clipping and be shown at both distortion- and utility-matched points.

The strongest domain-specific angle is the **cross-modal native-control design**. The audit evaluates a heterogeneous defense suite where image conclusions are anchored to an official Geiping/CIFAR-LeNet-Zhu control and tabular conclusions to official TabLeak/Adult control, rather than assuming that an image attack’s failure transfers to tabular data or vice versa. Seeded transforms are treated as non-DP mechanisms under an explicit key-holding-server threat model. Phrase results as case-study findings about the exact protocol and instrument; do not claim that all published defenses fail, that keyed transforms are universally insecure, or that a non-passing local attack demonstrates privacy.

## Three contribution bullets that do not overclaim

1. **Positive-control-first audit protocol.** We specify and apply four necessary evaluation checks for selected FL leakage defenses: undefended attack qualification, server-knowledge-aligned adaptation, input-space scoring against data-free baselines, and disclosed clipped DP comparisons at distortion- and utility-matched operating points.
2. **Native cross-modal reference controls.** We provide reproducible image and tabular reference controls using the official Geiping and TabLeak implementations in their corresponding native settings, then use those controls to delimit when conclusions about the studied defenses are interpretable.
3. **Threat-model and calibration case studies.** For the studied seeded transforms and aggregation/representation defenses, we document how server-held randomness, transmitted auxiliary state, attacker adaptation, and clipping/noise calibration change the measured leakage--utility conclusion; these are protocol-specific findings, not claims of formal DP or universal security/impossibility.
