# P34D independent reservation preflight

Before recovery freeze or confirmatory image execution, a new administrative
auditor independently traverses historical JSON record fields (including nested
job indices), regenerates the P29/P30 reserved populations, verifies mandatory
P33C/P34B manifests and hashes, and compares exclusions to the recovery driver.
It checks all four settings, 39 paired units each, 273 unique source-disjoint
CIFAR10/test IDs and the prespecified seed342600 order. It reads no image outcome
arrays. Any discrepancy stops before freeze; no replacement/tuning is permitted.
The receipt is additive and existing science sources/manifests are unchanged.
